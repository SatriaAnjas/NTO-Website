Our Journey

A continuing story shaped by Indonesian landscapes, responsible sourcing, and the people who make every relationship enduring.

Rooted in place.
Built to endure.

Nusa Terra Origin grows through a simple conviction: meaningful supply chains begin with respect for origin. From local producers and collection centres to our global partners, every step is an opportunity to create greater traceability, quality, and shared value.

01 / ORIGIN

Listening to the land

We begin close to the source, learning from the landscapes and communities that nurture Indonesia's botanicals.

02 / CRAFT

Building with care

Direct relationships and thoughtful processing help us protect quality while strengthening the people behind each product.

03 / HORIZON

Growing together

We connect responsible Indonesian origins with partners who share a long-term view of natural ingredients.

Who We Are

Rooted in Indonesia. Connected to the world.

Our origin

Nusa Terra Origin is a family-run company producing and exporting Indonesian essential oils and natural raw materials from source.

Our commitment

We build long-term partnerships with producers, distillers, and growers who share our commitment to quality and responsible sourcing.

Our Team

People behind the origins.

Close to the field

Our teams work closely with local producers, follow harvests, oversee production, and coordinate programs with our partners.

Growing together

We invest in the skills, capacity, and long-term future of the communities and producers who make each supply chain possible.

Supply Chain

From source to distillation.

Direct sourcing

We work directly with local farmers, collectors, and producers, maintaining visibility from raw material collection to processing.

Traceable quality

Each batch is selected, documented, and managed through controlled production before it reaches our export partners.
