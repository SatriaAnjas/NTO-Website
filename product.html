Vetiver

Garut, West Java, Indonesia

Vetiver from Garut is widely recognized for its earthy, woody, and refined character. At Nusa Terra Origin, we build direct relationships with producers in the region and manage quality from collection through processing, with both Conventional and Low Smoked variants available.

Patchouli Sumatra

Patchouli from Sumatra is a foundational ingredient in the global fragrance industry. Our regional collection centre in Banda Aceh allows us to work closely with producers at source, managing collection, grading, and preparation with traceability built into the process.
